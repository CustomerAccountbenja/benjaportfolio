About

Security-minded. Detail-driven.

I’m Benjamin Khoko, a final-year computer science student at DMI St John the Baptist University in Malawi. I design and build full-stack applications, with a particular interest in systems people have to trust with their data.

Across four end-to-end projects I’ve modelled file permissions and encrypted storage, benchmarked classification models for fraud detection, shipped a document-analysis workspace with more than 100 automated tests, and hardened an Ubuntu virtual machine against weak configurations, unnecessary services, loose permissions and outdated packages. I care about clean interfaces, honest evaluation, and code that someone else can actually run.
